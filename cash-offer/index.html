How it works

Who a cash offer works for

A move on a deadline

Relocation, a new job, a home already under contract. When the calendar matters more than squeezing out the last dollar.

A home that needs work

Deferred repairs, an older roof, a kitchen from another decade. Cash buyers take the property as it stands.

An inherited or vacant property

A house you do not live in and do not want to manage, insure, or keep paying taxes on while it waits for a buyer.

A rental with tenants

Sell without coordinating showings around someone else's schedule.

Cash offer questions

Do I have to accept the offer?

No. A cash offer is a written option, not a commitment. You can accept it, negotiate it, compare it against listing, or walk away. There is no fee to request one.

How fast can I close?

Cash sales skip the lender, so closing is set by you and the buyer, not by a bank. Many sellers who accept a cash offer close within a few weeks; if you need longer to move, that can be part of the terms.

Does my home need repairs first?

No. Cash offers are made on the property as it stands. You do not need to paint, repair, stage, or hold open houses.

Where do you make cash offers?

Jacksonville, Palm Coast, Fernandina Beach, Flagler Beach, Amelia Island, and the surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

How is the offer amount decided?

By a review of your property and the recent sales around it, adjusted for condition, location, and timing. You see the comparable sales alongside the offer, so you know how the number was built.

What is different about getting a cash offer through a brokerage?

You are working with a licensed Florida brokerage, not a single investor. You get the cash offer and the number you could expect by listing, side by side, and you choose the path that nets you more.

Would rather talk first?

One call, and you are talking to the person who will handle the sale.
