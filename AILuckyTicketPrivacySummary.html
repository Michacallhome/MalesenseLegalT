Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 7, 2026

Overview

AI Lucky Ticket is designed to work without requiring an account. We collect minimal data necessary to operate the app and improve stability.

Key Points: We do not sell your personal information. Data we process is not used to track you across apps or websites. Data is not linked to your identity because no account is required.

Data We Collect and Process

Identifiers

Device Identifier: We use a device identifier to manage your subscription status and purchase entitlements through our payment processor, RevenueCat. This identifier is an anonymous ID that is not linked to your personal identity.

  • Purpose: App functionality (subscription and purchase management)
  • Linked to your identity: No
  • Used for tracking: No

Diagnostics

Crash Data: We collect crash logs through Sentry to identify and fix app crashes. Crash reports include technical information about the error, device type, and operating system version. They do not include personal information.

  • Purpose: App functionality (stability improvement)
  • Linked to your identity: No
  • Used for tracking: No

Performance Data: We collect app performance metrics through Sentry to monitor app health and identify performance issues. This includes app launch times, screen load times, and error rates.

  • Purpose: App functionality (performance improvement)
  • Linked to your identity: No
  • Used for tracking: No

On-Device Storage

State Selection: Your selected state is stored on your device so the app remembers your choice.

Preferences: Settings such as your preferred options are stored locally on your device.

This on-device data is not transmitted to our servers.

Optional Location

If you choose to allow it, the app may request approximate location to suggest your state. Location access is optional. If you deny permission, you can select a state manually. We do not require or use precise location.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following service providers to operate the app:

RevenueCat

Purpose: Subscription and purchase entitlement management

Data processed: Anonymous app user identifier, purchase and subscription status, device identifiers, and transaction receipts as needed for entitlement validation

Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Sentry

Purpose: Crash reporting and performance monitoring

Data processed: Crash logs, device type, operating system version, app version, app performance metrics, and diagnostic events

Privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy

Apple

Purpose: In-app purchase processing

All purchases are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy practices apply to payment processing.

How We Use Information

We use the data described above only to:

  • Operate the app and provide its features
  • Process and manage subscriptions and purchases
  • Identify and fix crashes and bugs
  • Monitor and improve app performance

We do not use this data to track you across other apps or websites, build advertising profiles, or for any purpose other than operating and improving AI Lucky Ticket.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above (RevenueCat and Sentry) as necessary to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually required to protect it.

Retention

On-device settings remain until you change them or delete the app. Diagnostic data (crash reports and performance metrics) is retained by Sentry according to their data retention policies, typically for 90 days. Purchase entitlement data is retained by RevenueCat as long as you have an active subscription or for the period required to provide purchase history.

Children

AI Lucky Ticket is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will delete such information.

Your Choices

You have control over your data:

  • Location: You can disable location access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > AI Lucky Ticket
  • Diagnostics: You can opt out of sharing diagnostic data with app developers through iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements
  • Delete data: On-device preferences are deleted when you uninstall the app. To request deletion of any data held by our service providers, contact us at the email below
  • State selection: You can change your selected state in the app settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
