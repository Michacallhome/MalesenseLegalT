Northeast Florida Neighborhood Guides

Local guides for the communities we work in every week, from Jacksonville's historic districts to the Flagler County coast.

Jacksonville
Mandarin

Riverfront oaks, established neighborhoods, and some of the most sought-after schools on Jacksonville's Southside.
Jacksonville
Avondale

A historic riverside district of 1920s architecture, brick streets, and walkable dining along the Shoppes of Avondale.
Flagler County
Palm Coast

Canal-front living, master-planned sections, and one of the fastest-growing markets between Jacksonville and Daytona.
Amelia Island
Fernandina Beach

A Victorian-era downtown, working shrimp docks, and island living thirty minutes from Jacksonville's airport.
Flagler County
Flagler Beach

A small beach town on A1A with a fishing pier, no high-rises, and homes from cottages to canal-front.
Nassau County
Amelia Island

Thirteen miles of Atlantic beach with resort communities, historic Fernandina Beach, and a state park at each end.
