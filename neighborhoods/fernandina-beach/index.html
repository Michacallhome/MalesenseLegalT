Living in Fernandina Beach

Fernandina Beach anchors the north end of Amelia Island with a fifty-block historic district, a shrimping harbor, and a downtown that stays lively year round.

Housing ranges from island cottages and historic homes near Centre Street to newer construction closer to the beaches.

Neighborhood Highlights

  • Historic Centre Street downtown
  • Working waterfront and marina
  • Golf cart friendly island streets
  • Thirteen miles of beaches
