Fernandina Beach Homes for Sale

A Victorian-era downtown, working shrimp docks, and island living thirty minutes from Jacksonville's airport.

Living in Fernandina Beach

Fernandina Beach is the historic heart of Amelia Island, with a walkable downtown of Victorian storefronts along Centre Street, a marina on the Amelia River, and Fort Clinch State Park at the island's northern tip.

Buyers get small-town character with beach access minutes away, and the housing mix runs from restored historic homes to newer subdivisions on the island's south end.

Where it is

Fernandina Beach is the city at the north end of Amelia Island, in Nassau County, about thirty to forty minutes northeast of Jacksonville's airport. Centre Street runs from the Amelia River marina toward the Atlantic and carries the Victorian downtown that gives the town its identity. Fort Clinch State Park anchors the northern tip, and Main Beach sits at the Atlantic end of town.

The homes

Three markets in one town. The historic district has restored Victorian and early 1900s homes on tree-lined streets close to downtown. The beach side has cottages, elevated homes, and condos near the ocean. South of town, newer neighborhoods and Amelia Park offer 2000s and newer construction with sidewalks and shorter walks to everything.

Coastal insurance, elevation, and construction year matter here more than in inland Jacksonville, so the comparable sales tell a clearer story than any online estimate. Sellers with older beachside or historic homes that need work are the ones who most often ask about a cash offer.

Who buys here

Second-home buyers, retirees, remote workers who want a walkable town, and Jacksonville families trading a commute for the island. The annual shrimp festival, the Saturday market, and the fact that you can walk from dinner to the marina explain a lot of the demand.

Neighborhood highlights

  • Historic downtown on Centre Street
  • Fort Clinch State Park and the beaches
  • Marina and riverfront dining
  • Thirty minutes to Jacksonville's airport

Fernandina Beach questions

Is Fernandina Beach the same as Amelia Island?

Fernandina Beach is the city at the north end of Amelia Island. The island also includes resort and residential communities to the south, such as Amelia Island Plantation and the areas around the Ritz-Carlton. We cover both.

What should I know before buying near the beach here?

Elevation, flood zone, roof age, and insurance cost shape the true monthly payment on coastal homes. We pull those details before you make an offer, not after.
