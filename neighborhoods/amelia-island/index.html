Living in Amelia Island

The south end of Amelia Island is defined by its resort corridor, championship golf, and protected dune lines, with neighborhoods tucked under maritime oaks.

Buyers choose the island for its balance of luxury inventory, natural preserves, and a fifteen-minute drive to Fernandina's historic downtown.

Neighborhood Highlights

  • Resort corridor and golf communities
  • Protected dunes and beach access
  • Maritime oak canopy
  • Luxury and second-home market
