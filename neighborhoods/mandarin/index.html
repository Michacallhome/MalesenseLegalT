Mandarin Homes for Sale

Riverfront oaks, established neighborhoods, and some of the most sought-after schools on Jacksonville's Southside.

Living in Mandarin

Mandarin stretches along the east bank of the St. Johns River in southern Jacksonville, known for its canopy roads, mature lots, and a mix of established subdivisions and riverfront estates.

Buyers are drawn to the school zones, quick access to I-295, and a quieter pace that still sits twenty minutes from downtown and the beaches.

Where it is

Mandarin sits at the southern end of Jacksonville, bounded by the St. Johns River to the west and Julington Creek to the south, where Duval County meets St. Johns County. San Jose Boulevard runs through the middle of it and carries most of the shopping and dining, while Mandarin Road and Loretto Road keep the old canopy-oak character the neighborhood is known for.

Downtown Jacksonville is about twenty minutes north on I-295 and the Buckman Bridge puts the Westside and Orange Park within reach. The beaches are thirty to forty minutes east.

The homes

Most of Mandarin was built between the 1970s and the early 2000s, so the typical home is a single-story or two-story house on a lot larger than you get in the newer parts of the county, often with mature trees. Riverfront and creekfront homes along Mandarin Road and the Julington Creek side are the top of the market and rarely trade.

Because the housing stock is established, condition drives price here more than almost anywhere else in Jacksonville. Updated kitchens, roofs, and HVAC separate the homes that sell in a weekend from the ones that sit. That is also why Mandarin is one of the neighborhoods where a cash offer can make sense for a seller who does not want to renovate first.

Who buys here

Families who want the school zones and a yard, boaters who want water access without leaving the city, and buyers moving up from the newer subdivisions who want trees and a neighborhood with a history. Walter Jones Historical Park and the Mandarin Museum on the river are the kind of thing people mention when they explain why they stayed.

Neighborhood highlights

  • St. Johns riverfront access
  • Canopy oak streets and mature lots
  • Established schools and parks
  • Quick I-295 access
