Flagler Beach Homes for Sale

An old-Florida beach town with cinnamon sand, a walkable pier district, and no high-rise skyline.

Living in Flagler Beach

Flagler Beach keeps a low-rise, small-town character rare on the Florida coast, with A1A running directly along the dunes and a pier district of local restaurants.

Inventory mixes elevated coastal homes, beachside cottages, and a limited supply of oceanfront condos, which keeps well-priced listings moving quickly.

Neighborhood Highlights

  • Uncrowded cinnamon-sand beaches
  • Walkable pier and restaurant district
  • No high-rise development
  • Scenic A1A frontage
