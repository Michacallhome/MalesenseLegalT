Investor deal flow questions

Are these on the MLS?

No. This is off-market and wholesale deal flow: assignable contracts, light rehabs, and value-add properties that move before they ever reach a public listing. If you want on-market inventory too, use our regular home search.

What do I have to give you before I see anything?

Just your buy box: price range, area, and strategy (fix and flip, buy and hold, land). No fee, no commitment, and your information is never sold.

Do you charge a fee to send me deals?

No. If you close on a property through Call Home Realty, standard brokerage compensation applies, disclosed before any offer, same as any transaction.

What if the deal isn't in Florida?

Call Home Realty is a licensed Florida brokerage. If deal flow surfaces a property outside Florida, we say so plainly and can refer you to a local licensed agent in that market rather than advise on it ourselves.

How fast do deals move?

Off-market inventory this priced under market moves in days, sometimes hours. Buyers on our list get first look based on how closely a deal matches their stated buy box.

Selling to an investor instead?

If you're the one selling, get a written cash offer within 10 days or less, with the listing price beside it so you can compare.

Would rather talk first?

One call, and you are talking to the person who will handle it.
