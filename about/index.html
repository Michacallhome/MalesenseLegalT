Two Licenses, One Point of Contact

Most transactions stall in the gap between the agent and the lender. Micha Kiel works both sides of that gap. As a licensed Realtor with Call Home Realty and a licensed mortgage professional, Micha reads a pre-approval the way an underwriter does and structures offers that survive financing.
